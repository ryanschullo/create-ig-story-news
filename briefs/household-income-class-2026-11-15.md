MoneyLion defines a middle-income band from two-thirds to twice each state's median household income, then divides that band into thirds. Its lower-middle band is therefore two-thirds to ten-ninths of that state's median. The graphic accurately calls this the top of the lower-middle band, rather than a measure of living costs or disposable income.
