## Source reading and factual scope

The full public X page and official syndication metadata were read. The post says that households aged 75 and older had mean net worth about 5% above the overall household average in 1983 and 55% above it in 2022. Households under 35 moved from 21% of the overall average in 1983 to 16% in 2022.

The graphic expresses these approximate ratios consistently as percentages of the overall household mean: **105% → 155%** for age 75+, and **21% → 16%** for under 35. **100% means average household net worth.** These are not shares of total national wealth, median values, income measures, or percentage changes in dollar wealth. A decline relative to the overall average does not establish that younger households' net worth fell in nominal dollars. The two dates compare age groups, not the same cohort followed over time. The graphic makes no causal claim.

The chart is titled “Net Worth of Youngest and Oldest Age Groups in the US” and labels both series “Mean net worth relative to average household.” Its credit is “Researcher’s calculations using data from the Survey of Consumer Finances.” The post/chart does not identify that researcher. The two chart panels use different y-axis ranges, which are retained. The post's figures and the displayed series agree; calculations from primary microdata have not been independently replicated. Attribution remains to the post and its chart, not to an independently verified official Federal Reserve chart.
