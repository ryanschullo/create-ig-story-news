## Essential comparison context

The American Dream basket includes **family and household milestones**: a home,
two cars, raising two children and sending them to college, retirement,
healthcare, a wedding, pets, and annual vacations. Fox Business specifically
describes retirement costs for a **married couple over 20 years** and two new
cars for the household.

The earnings benchmark represents **one worker**, not total household income.
Keep the user's requested $3.6 million, $4.1 million and $1.2 million figures,
while making that mismatch explicit. A two-earner household is not represented
by this income benchmark. Do not turn the illustrative comparison into a claim
that every worker or typical household is necessarily $1.2 million short.
