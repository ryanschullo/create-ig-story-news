## Verified source reading

The full Yahoo Finance article was accessible and read, including its three figure descriptions. Yahoo quotes Truist Wealth chief investment officer Keith Lerner's Thursday, November 5 note: **the S&P 500 technology sector, represented by XLK, traded at about 17 times expected earnings, versus about 18 times for the broader S&P 500**. This is the first relative valuation discount since 2017. The article gives no exact quote observation time; the graphic identifies the November 5 note rather than labeling these figures a November 6 closing snapshot. These are forward/expected-earnings multiples, not trailing multiples, returns or share prices. “Technology sector” is not interchangeable with the Nasdaq Composite, Nasdaq-100, all AI companies or the semiconductor index.

The article separately discusses the S&P 500's historical performance following **33 streaks of four or more weekly losses since 1990**. Its chart shows median subsequent returns: 1 month +1.9%, 3 months +6.0%, 6 months +10.3%, and 1 year +16.5%, compared with +1.2%, +3.2%, +5.7%, and +11.9% after any week. The article says the index was higher about 80% of the time three months after these streaks and identifies the dot-com bust and 2008 financial crisis as exceptions, when losses continued for another year. This study conditions on losing streaks, **not on technology trading at a valuation discount**. The graphic clearly separates the valuation comparison from the historical-return chart and retains the exception context. Historical medians are not forecasts; no buy recommendation or causal link is asserted.

The source contains a stock heat map, a semiconductor losing-streak chart and this S&P 500 historical-return chart. It contains no original technology-versus-S&P valuation chart. The selected original chart is preserved, not mislabeled as a valuation series. Underlying return calculations were not independently recomputed; attribution remains to Yahoo Finance.
