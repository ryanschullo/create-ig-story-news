# Stock options — Instagram countdown and link template

## Request and use

The user requested a stock-options-focused graphic with space for an Instagram timer in the middle and a link below it. This is a reusable promotional Story background, not an article summary or a market-data report. No event name, date, countdown end time or URL was supplied; none is invented or baked into the image.

Upload the final 1080 × 1920 PNG to Instagram Stories, add Instagram's native Countdown sticker in the large central space, and add its native Link sticker in the smaller space below. The PNG itself is static and does not contain a working timer or clickable link. Set the countdown title/end time and destination URL inside Instagram.

## Design

- Palette: ink, plum and indigo with restrained teal and warm gold accents.
- Motifs: dimensional smoked-glass slabs, abstract trading forms and light ribbons around the perimeter; they do not encode market prices, financial results or a trading signal.
- Compact one-line brand: MONEYBERG NEWS BRIEF, Lato Black 900, 35 px.
- Separate category: OPTIONS INVESTING, Lato Black 900, 33 px.
- Hero: STOCK / OPTIONS, Lato Black 900, approximately 120 px.
- Supporting copy: “Strategy starts with timing.” in Georgia 400, 38 px.
- All authored content fits x104–961 and y164–1536; all current font minima apply.
- The countdown interior is reserved around x200–880, y820–1100; its center is approximately 50% across and 50% down the Story.
- The link interior is reserved around x240–840, y1210–1320; its center is approximately 50% across and 66% down the Story.
- Sticker areas contain no placeholder labels, digits, mock URLs or other authored content. Faint surrounding outlines guide placement without competing with Instagram's own stickers.
- Use only the unchanged original `assets/moneyberg-coin-original.png`, uniformly scaled and physically integrated with the background recess, wholly above y1440 and clear of both sticker spaces.
- No date, event commitment, investment recommendation, profit promise, source citation or background-production footer is added.
