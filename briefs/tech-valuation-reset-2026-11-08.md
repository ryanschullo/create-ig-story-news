## Verification and scope

The complete Moomoo article was read in Chromium after the page required JavaScript. The complete original Chinese Cailian Press article was independently retrieved and read. Its numerical claims agree with the English translation and with the previously verified Yahoo Finance report, but this graphic is sourced to the user's selected Moomoo/Cailian report.

The article attributes the figures to **Keith Lerner, chief investment officer at Truist Wealth**:

- At the **October 2025 peak**, the **S&P 500 technology sector's forward P/E** was **32×**.
- In the **November 2026 report**, the sector trades at **approximately 17× expected earnings**.
- The broader **S&P 500 trades at approximately 18×**.
- Technology therefore trades at a lower forward P/E than the broader index for the **first time since 2017**.

The article does not provide an exact valuation observation day or time. The graphic labels the latter figure **NOV. 2026 REPORT**, not “November 8 close,” “today's close” or a live quote. The ratio is price relative to expected earnings. These numbers are not stock prices, earnings growth, investment returns, or a statement that the sector's share price halved. No exact percentage decline is calculated from rounded multiples. The sector is the S&P 500 technology sector, not the Nasdaq or every AI stock.

The focus is the **32× → ~17× valuation reset**, distinct from the preceding Yahoo Finance story's historical-return chart. This graphic makes no prediction or buy recommendation. It omits the article's unrelated historical losing-streak backtest, market-wrap figures and machine-translated introductory chronology. The original Chinese introduction correctly places post-election euphoria before Thursday's selloff; the English rendering is less clear, so that detail is not carried into this summary.
