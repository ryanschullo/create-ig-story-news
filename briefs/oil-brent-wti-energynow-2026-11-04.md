## Timestamp discrepancy and safe presentation

- Article header: “Report Date & Time: November 4, 2026 – 5:29 a.m. MST – Using Trading View Prices”.
- Body says price quotes come from Reuters at **3:34 a.m. MST**, not independently verifiable live chart trades.
- Body also retains an inconsistent **4:29 a.m. MST** preparation-time reference.
- Therefore use the explicitly attributed quotation time (**4:34 a.m. CST**), not either preparation time. Label clearly **INTRADAY** / **REPORTED SNAPSHOT**.
- Use “reported prices” and/or “approximately”; do not claim independently verified Reuters transactions from this research. We independently read EnergyNow's account; direct Reuters text was inaccessible (401).
- U.S. daylight saving time ended November 1, 2026. For November 4 the client Chicago timezone is **CST**, not CDT.
