Additional options beyond `article-options-2026-10-25.md` and the completed
Apollo, week-ahead calendar, and American Dream graphics. All ten publisher
pages and full article bodies were read. Publication dates are October 25 in
America/Chicago; some publisher URLs use October 26. Sunday features and
analysis are identified rather than presented as newly occurring events.

## 1. Home sellers cut asking prices as the housing market stalls

[Associated Press via Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/25/housing-market-home-prices-buyers-sellers-supply-mortgage-rates/)

- Published October 25, 14:16 UTC / 9:16 a.m. Chicago. Alex Veiga/AP.
- Realtor.com figures cited in the article: about 21% of September listings
  had their original asking prices cut, a four-year high. National median
  listing price fell 1.2% year over year to $419,250, the eleventh consecutive
  monthly year-over-year decline.
- Redfin's cited September analysis finds about 63% more sellers than buyers
  across the 50 largest U.S. metropolitan areas.
- Listing-price reductions are not the same as a 21% fall in sale prices.
  The 63% comparison is for those metropolitan areas, not every U.S. market.
- Same AP story also read through the Boston Herald; do not count both
  syndications as separate options.

## 3. Could repricing America's gold reserves backfire?

[Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/25/us-largest-gold-reserves-1-trillion-mark-to-market-debt-treasury-bond-yields/)

- Jason Ma; published October 25, 17:57:01 UTC / 12:57 p.m. Chicago.
- About 261.5 million ounces of U.S. gold are carried at the statutory
  $42.22 an ounce, just over $11 billion in total. The article compares that
  with roughly $1 trillion at a market price around $4,000 an ounce.
- Fidelity's Jurrien Timmer argues that revaluation could weaken the dollar
  and raise the Treasury term premium. This is a hypothetical policy analysis;
  congressional approval would be required, and the administration previously
  said the idea was not under serious consideration.
- Do not describe revaluation as an implemented policy, free debt elimination,
  or a guaranteed market outcome. The gold value is the article's snapshot.

  aggregate Georgia voter counts without human authorization. The company
  says it has taken steps to block this behavior.
