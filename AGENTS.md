# Instagram Story graphics

For creating or revising news graphics, read and follow
`.agents/skills/ig-story-news/SKILL.md`.

The user's marked content area is a required layout constraint. Fit every part of
the news content inside it, including the full headline, image, body text, chart
axes and legend, and source attribution. Never satisfy the constraint by clipping
content or making text too small to read.

Always use Lato Black (weight 900) for headlines and labels, and Georgia for
body text, captions, and source attribution. Follow the skill's full font
assignments; do not silently substitute other fonts.

Use `MONEYBERG NEWS BRIEF` as the recurring brand label for new graphics and
requested revisions. Keep all three words on one compact line in Lato Black
(weight 900), secondary to the story headline, around 28–34 px at 1080 px canvas
width. Adjust spacing and size to fit rather than wrapping. Keep it inside the
content bounds. Leave historical exports unchanged unless requested.

Daily U.S. market wrap graphics must show the Nasdaq Composite, S&P 500, and
Dow Jones Industrial Average results for the same trading date. Verify each
daily percentage change and closing level; add point changes only when verified.
Never substitute Nasdaq-100 silently, invent missing data, or derive point
changes from rounded values. Label nonfinal snapshots as intraday. This rule
applies to broad market wraps, not every unrelated news graphic.

Give each subject a distinct design, palette, layout, and relevant background
imagery. Oil stories use a petroleum/charcoal/amber industrial treatment; other
subjects need their own visual approach. Power and infrastructure stories use
charcoal, steel blue, restrained electric yellow, and relevant grid imagery.
Always use the user's original uploaded gold Moneyberg coin as the only approved
coin artwork. Preserve its exact portrait, rim lettering, and circular
proportions; do not redraw it or use generated variants as substitutes. Integrate
it subtly near an edge or in the lower background while subject-specific imagery
remains primary. The original is currently a conversation reference, not an
archived repository asset. If it is inaccessible in a future task, obtain the
original file or source URL before adding the coin. Decorative backgrounds may
extend outside the content area, but all added text stays inside.
