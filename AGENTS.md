# Instagram Story graphics

For creating or revising news graphics, read and follow
`.agents/skills/ig-story-news/SKILL.md`.

The user's marked content area is a required layout constraint. Fit every part of
the news content inside it, including the full headline, image, body text, chart
axes and legend, and source attribution. Never satisfy the constraint by clipping
content or making text too small to read.

Always use Lato Black (weight 900) for headlines and labels, and Georgia for
body text, captions, and source attribution. Follow the skill's full font
assignments; do not silently substitute other fonts.

Use the user's supplied Moneyberg coin/emblem as subtle decorative background
branding. Preserve its proportions and integral artwork lettering; keep it low
contrast so the news remains readable. Decorative emblem imagery may extend
outside the content area, but all added text must stay inside it.
